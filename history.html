1874 – 2026

History

From a garden-party game on English lawns to electronic line calling, these are the moments that shaped modern tennis.

  1. 1874

    Wingfield patents lawn tennis

    Major Walter Clopton Wingfield sold boxed sets of a game he called "Sphairistikè", splitting the modern game off from real tennis.

  2. 1877

    The first Wimbledon

    The All England Club held its first Championships. The 15-30-40 scoring and court dimensions were fixed around this time.

  3. 1881

    U.S. National Championships begin

    The forerunner of the US Open. It moved to Flushing Meadows and hard courts in 1978.

  4. 1891

    French Championships begin

    Open only to members of French clubs until 1925. Stade Roland Garros opened in 1928.

  5. 1900

    Davis Cup founded

    Started as a USA vs. Great Britain match. The women’s competition, now the Billie Jean King Cup, followed in 1963.

  6. 1905

    Australasian Championships

    The forerunner of the Australian Open. It moved from grass to hard courts at Melbourne Park in 1988.

  7. 1938

    Don Budge wins the first calendar Grand Slam

    All four majors in one year. Among men, only Rod Laver (1962, 1969) has done it since.

  8. 1968

    The Open Era begins

    Professionals were allowed into the majors, putting amateurs and pros in the same draws.

  9. 1972–73

    ATP, WTA and computer rankings

    The ATP formed in 1972 and Billie Jean King led the founding of the WTA in 1973. ATP computer rankings started that year, and the US Open paid men and women equally for the first time.

  10. 1988

    Steffi Graf’s Golden Slam

    All four majors plus Olympic gold in Seoul, the year tennis returned as a full Olympic sport.

  11. 2006

    Hawk-Eye challenges arrive

    Electronic review debuted in Miami and at the US Open and changed how calls are disputed.

  12. 2022

    One final-set tiebreak for all majors

    All four Grand Slams adopted a 10-point tiebreak at 6–6 in the deciding set.

  13. 2025

    Wimbledon replaces line judges

    After 148 years, every court switched to electronic line calling.

  14. 2026

    Alcaraz completes the career Grand Slam

    He won the Australian Open at 22. Later that year Zverev won Roland Garros and the US Open back to back.
